The source manifest's `access: downloaded` values record research-time access. `local_path: null` means the original is not included in the public project. The ontology and observation rules are project-authored interpretations with the limits recorded in their files; consult the original publisher before applying a source as a professional standard.

Development reports, acquisition logs, extraction notes, and downloaded originals are excluded from the public tree. No test images or generated-image results are included.
